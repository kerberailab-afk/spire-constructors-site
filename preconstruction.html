Five services, one number

What pre-construction covers.

Everything that has to be true on paper before it can be true in the field.

Conceptual and detailed estimating

From an early order-of-magnitude number to a line-item budget, so you know what you are really building before you fund it.

Value engineering

We find the cost without cutting the quality: smarter assemblies, better sequencing and material choices that protect your number and your finish.

Constructability and design review

We read the set the way the field will. Conflicts, gaps and buildability problems get caught on paper, where they cost a redline instead of a change order.

Scheduling and phasing

A realistic, honest schedule, including how to phase around an occupied site, a tenant or an opening date.

Permitting and entitlement strategy

With zoning and land-development experience in-house, we map the permitting and entitlement path early, so approvals do not blindside your timeline.

Why it pays for itself

A budget you can defend.

A number you can defend before you commit capital beats a hopeful one you discover in the field. Pre-construction turns a wished-for schedule into a buildable one and a drawing set into one the field can build from, while a change is still a pencil mark and not a change order.
