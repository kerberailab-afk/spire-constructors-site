How we help

Five ways to put a builder on your side.

Each one is a service on its own. Most owners start with one and add the rest as the job gets real.

01 · Owner's Representation

Owner's Representation

We represent your interests through the build: coordinating, questioning, and holding the team to the budget, schedule and quality you were promised.

02 · Bid Leveling

Bid Leveling & GC Selection

We level competing proposals apples to apples, expose the gaps and where the numbers do not add up, and help you choose the right builder for the right reasons.

03 · Oversight

Budget & Schedule Oversight

Independent eyes on the numbers and the timeline, so slippage gets flagged early, while you can still do something about it.

04 · Draw Review

Draw & Pay-Application Review

We review pay apps and draw requests against actual progress, so you are funding work that is genuinely in place.

05 · Advisory

Project Advisory & Audit

A second opinion when a build is off the rails, or a sanity check before you commit. Honest, builder-side counsel.

Who it's for

Every contractor gives you three glowing references. We read the schedule, the pay apps and the dirt.

Developers, institutional owners, and first-time owners on large projects who want someone with real construction scars reading the dirt for them, not a consultant who has never poured a footing.

The schedule

Not the one in the proposal. The one on the wall of the trailer, with the float used up and the inspections still to come. We tell you what is real and what is hope.

The pay apps

Line by line against what is actually in place. Stored materials, retainage, lien releases from every sub. You release money for work you can walk through.

The dirt

Site work is where budgets go to die: unsuitable soils, utility conflicts, dewatering nobody priced. We have dug the trenches. We know what to ask before the excavator arrives.
