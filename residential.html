Straight answers

The questions homeowners actually ask.

Can I build an ADU on my lot? +
ADU rules are set by your city or county, not the state. Florida's proposed statewide ADU bill (SB 48) passed the Senate but did not become law in 2026. We check your local ordinance before we draw anything, and we tell you what it allows before you spend a dollar on design.
How do you handle change orders? +
In writing, priced, and signed by you before the work happens. Never after. If nothing changes, nothing changes on your bill.
Who runs my project day to day? +
One of the owners. Bill or Gary is on your site and on your phone from the first estimate to the final walkthrough.
Are you licensed, insured and bonded? +
Spire Constructors is a Florida Certified General Contractor, license CGC1539625, current and active. Look it up on MyFloridaLicense.com. We carry general liability and workers' compensation and will send certificates naming you as holder. Ask us about bonding for your project and we will answer in writing.
How do I know your subs get paid? +
Lien releases with every draw, from us and from every sub. You never pay for work that is not in place, and nobody can lien your property for a bill we owe.
