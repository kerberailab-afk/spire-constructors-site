Where the law stands

Your city writes the rules.

ADU rules are set by your city or county, not the state. Florida's proposed statewide ADU bill (SB 48) passed the Senate but didn't become law in 2026. We check your local ordinance before we draw anything. If a statewide bill returns in 2027, we'll update this guide.

  • Size limits, setbacks and height come from your local ordinance
  • Some cities set a 30-day minimum on rentals, and some limit who may rent an ADU
  • Owner-occupancy and parking rules are local too, and they differ city to city
  • We run the permit and inspection path start to finish

Straight answers

The ADU questions owners actually ask.

Does Florida allow ADUs statewide? +
No. ADU rules are set by your city or county, not the state. Florida's proposed statewide ADU bill (SB 48) passed the Senate but didn't become law in 2026. We check your local ordinance before we draw anything.
What does a detached ADU cost? +
Most detached ADUs in South Florida land between $210,000 and $310,000 all-in for about 600 SF. Impact fees vary by county and are part of the number we give you. We price yours to the dollar before you commit.
How much rent can an ADU bring in? +
An estimated $15,000 to $30,000 a year in gross rent at 2026 one-bedroom market rates, before vacancy, taxes, insurance and maintenance, and subject to your city's rental rules.
Can I rent it short-term? +
Some Florida cities set a 30-day minimum on rentals, and some limit who may rent an ADU. We check your city's rules before design, so you know before you commit.
Who runs my project day to day? +
One of the two owners, from the first estimate to the final walkthrough. On your site, and on the other end of the phone.
