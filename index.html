Add an ADU. Add income. Add value.

Most detached ADUs in South Florida run $210,000 to $310,000 all-in for about 600 square feet, built to hurricane code. A one-bedroom can bring in an estimated $15,000 to $30,000 a year in gross rent at 2026 market rates, before vacancy, taxes, insurance and maintenance, and subject to your city's rental rules. Your city or county sets those rules, and we handle them.

Straight answers

The questions owners actually ask.

Your quote is higher than another bid. Why? +
Low bids in Florida are usually incomplete bids. The gap comes back as change orders once your options are gone. We price the whole scope up front, in writing, and show you the breakdown.
How do you handle change orders? +
In writing, priced, and signed by you before the work happens. Never after. If nothing changes, nothing changes on your bill.
Who runs my project day to day? +
One of the two owners, from the first estimate to the final walkthrough. On your site, and on the other end of the phone.
Are you licensed, insured and bonded? +
Spire Constructors is a Florida Certified General Contractor, license CGC1539625, current and active. Look it up on MyFloridaLicense.com. We carry general liability and workers' compensation and will send certificates naming you as holder. Ask us about bonding for your project and we'll answer in writing.
Can you work in an occupied building? +
Yes. Medical offices, restaurants and retail stay open while we work. We phase the job around your hours, control dust and noise, and sequence inspections so your opening date holds.
How many projects do you take on at once? +
Only as many as two owners can personally run. If we can't give your project senior attention, we tell you before you sign, not after.
How do I know your subs get paid? +
Lien releases with every draw, from us and from every sub. You never pay for work that isn't in place, and nobody can lien your property for a bill we owe.
Do you build outside Florida? +
Our owners have delivered projects in Florida, Kentucky, Arkansas, Missouri and New York. Spire is licensed statewide in Florida and takes select projects nationally.
