The project

A ground-up modern home in the heart of Delray.

On a deep infill lot on SW 2nd Avenue, around the corner from Sundy Village in downtown Delray Beach, Spire is building a three-story modern single-family residence with a detached rear accessory dwelling unit.

The design uses a warm, modern material palette: crisp white stucco, wood-clad accents, vertical louvers and rooftop greenery. The program is a 3,188 SF main house across three floors, plus a 427 SF rooftop lounge, plus an 883 SF detached ADU. That's 4,071 SF of living area across house and ADU, on a 7,841 SF lot. A complete ground-up build, main house and ADU, with Spire as general contractor.

  • Ground-up new construction
