The brief

The listing people scroll back up for.

Our client is an investor. His goal was simple to say and hard to deliver: a Delray Beach short-term rental that stands apart from the look-alike rentals around it. In a crowded market, nice is not enough. It has to be the listing people scroll back up to book.

So we treated the build like a product. We took the property end to end: a full renovation of the house, a resort-style backyard with a custom pool, large-scale murals by a commissioned artist, and a clean, photo-ready coastal interior. Every decision was made through one lens: will a guest choose this one?

  • Custom in-ground pool
  • Resort backyard and pergola lounge
  • Large-scale artist murals
  • Color-changing pool and landscape lighting
  • Designer kitchen and baths
  • Turf, landscaping, putting green and cornhole
  • Turnkey, listing-ready handover

How it came together

From ordinary house to standout rental.

01 · The build

A backyard worth booking for

The centerpiece is the backyard. We excavated and built a custom in-ground pool, commissioned large-scale murals along the fence line, and finished it with a pergola lounge, turf and a putting green: a resort-style outdoor space built to be the reason a guest picks this house.

02 · Inside

Bright, modern, photo-ready

We took the interior down to the studs and rebuilt it as a light, coastal space: an open kitchen and living room, new baths, and bedrooms that photograph as well as they sleep.

03 · The standout

The details that set it apart

This is what separates the property from the listings around it: commissioned murals, a resort pool, color-changing pool and landscape lighting, turf, and outdoor games. An outdoor space that photographs the way it lives.
