How we work

Level. Plumb. Square. On the number.

The same four rules on every job, from a medical build-out to a backyard ADU.

01

One price, in writing

We price the whole scope up front and show you the breakdown. If something changes, you see the cost and sign off before any work starts.

02

Daily logs you can read

Every workday ends with a log in your client portal: what got done, photos from the site, and what's next.

03

Owners on every job

The people who own the company run your project, start to finish. You won't get handed off to a junior super.

04

Permits, handled

We pull the permits, schedule the inspections and deal with the city. If your building is open for business, it stays open while we work.
